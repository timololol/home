Getting Involved.

There are many ways to connect, grow and serve at BWMC. Explore our groups and ministries to find your place.

01 · Children

Kids Club

A fun and welcoming place for kids to learn about Jesus.

Kids Club helps children discover the Bible, build friendships and grow in their understanding of God's love in a safe and engaging environment.

Age ranges

  • Bubs & Tots: 0 - 3 years old
  • Kindie: 3 - 5 years old
  • Primary: 5 - 12 years old

11:45am - 12:45pm on Sundays during the school term

Contact: Vanessa Kerk

02 · Youth

LinC

Connecting with friends and discovering what it means to follow Jesus.

LinC is a place for high school students to connect, explore their faith and navigate life together. It's an opportunity to build meaningful friendships in a supportive Christian community.

Age range

  • Grades 6 - 12

7:30pm on Fridays during the school term

Contact: Pinky Hui
